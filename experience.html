Our Story

Born to Challenge the Ordinary

Established in July 2021 along Pendatun Avenue in General Santos City, Wild Shakes Café was born from a passion to challenge a saturated beverage market and give people something truly extraordinary.

We craft premium fruit shakes and milkshakes packed with bold, unique flavors — combining real, fresh fruits with our signature milk formula that keeps every drink thick, rich, and creamy for hours without diluting. Beyond our signature beverages, we offer an inviting space to relax, enjoy great food, and experience top-tier customer service.
