Common Questions

Franchise FAQ

Everything you need to know before you apply. Don't see your question? Reach out directly - we're happy to help.

Investment costs vary by location, size, and build-out scope. Reach out via the form below and our franchise team will walk you through exact figures during your consultation.

Franchisees pay a 5% monthly royalty fee and a 2% marketing fund contribution, both based on gross monthly sales. These fees fund continuous menu R&D, national marketing campaigns, and ongoing operational support - there are no hidden charges.

Most franchisees go from signed agreement to grand opening in 10-12 weeks, depending on construction timelines and site readiness. Our team manages the process closely to keep things on schedule.

Yes. Each franchise location is granted a protected radius based on local population density and existing branch coverage, so you won't be competing with another Wildshakes branch for the same customers.

Every franchisee and their core staff complete hands-on training at an existing Wildshakes branch before opening - covering drink and food preparation, POS systems, inventory management, and customer service standards. After launch, our regional support team stays in touch for ongoing coaching, and we're always a call away.

No prior café experience is required. Many of our most successful franchisees came from completely different industries. What matters most is your commitment to the brand standards and willingness to be hands-on, especially in your first few months.

We're actively looking for franchise partners in Mindanao, Visayas, and Metro Manila, with priority given to high-foot-traffic commercial areas, university belts, and mixed-use developments. Tell us your target city in the form below and we'll let you know what's available.
